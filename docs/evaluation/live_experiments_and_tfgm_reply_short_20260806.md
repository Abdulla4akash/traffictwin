# Live experiments and TfGM reply — short summary

## Live BODS experiments

| Session | Snapshots | Active buses | Cadence p50/p90 | Progression |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Night | 15 | 41 | 68/75 s | 6.290 m/s |
| Dawn | 52 | 1,162 | 67/76 s | 4.441 m/s |
| Morning peak | 51 | 1,431 | 66/75 s | 3.518 m/s |
| Evening peak | 83 | 1,522 | 66/75 s | 3.610 m/s |

- Feed cadence stayed stable across a roughly 36-fold active-fleet range.
- Morning-peak bus progression was about **44% lower than at night**.
- This is observational: bus progression is not general road speed, and no causal effect is proven.
- Cross-operator vehicle-ID collisions were found; operator-scoped identity prevented false tracks.
- B-BUS reused real mobility, but its tasks, RSUs and computing outcomes were simulated.

The morning movement table uses 51 promoted snapshots and 1,431 linked buses; the wider verified
session contained 52 quarantines and 1,433 active buses.

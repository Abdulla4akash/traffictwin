# Participant Information And Consent Draft

Status: draft; replace bracketed fields and obtain approval before use.

The study evaluates a research-software workflow for traffic and vehicular-edge-computing
experiments. Participation is voluntary and should take approximately 30–45 minutes. You may skip
any question or stop without giving a reason.

The study records a random participant code, broad experience categories, task outcomes, timing,
ratings or coded notes, and optional non-identifying comments. It does not require your name,
email, staff/student number, precise employer, or confidential simulator data.

Your data will be stored in [approved location], accessible to [researcher and supervisor], and
deleted by [date/retention rule]. You may request withdrawal using your participant code until
[anonymisation cutoff]. Contact [researcher email] or [supervisor email] for questions.

Consent statements:

- I have read the approved participant information.
- I understand participation is voluntary and I may stop.
- I understand what data will be collected and how it will be used.
- I understand the withdrawal deadline and process.
- I agree to take part in the study.
- Optional, only if separately approved: I agree to audio/screen recording.

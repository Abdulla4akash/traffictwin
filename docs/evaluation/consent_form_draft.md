# Consent Form (Draft)

**Status: PROPOSED — unsigned, unapproved, and not submitted.** No ethics committee has seen
this, no supervisor has signed it, and no reference number exists. **No participant has consented
to anything, and no consent record exists.** An agent never completes, signs, dates, or witnesses
this form, and never fabricates a participant, a code, or a response.

This is the printable consent half of `user_evaluation_instrument_draft.md` §3, split out because
an ethics submission attaches an information sheet and a consent form as two separate documents.
The companion document is `participant_information_sheet_draft.md`, which the participant reads
first.

Every value here is carried from the proposed-value table in `ethics_application_draft.md`. That
draft is the authority: if the two ever disagree, the ethics draft wins and this file is wrong.

## Please tick each box

The session does not begin until every statement below is ticked. If you are not willing to tick
one of them, please say so — you do not need to give a reason, and there is no consequence.

- [ ] I have read and understood the participant information sheet for this study.
- [ ] I have had the opportunity to ask questions, and they have been answered.
- [ ] I understand that taking part is **voluntary**, that I may stop at any time without giving
      a reason, and that stopping has no consequence for me.
- [ ] I understand that **no screen, audio, or video recording is made** at any point.
- [ ] I understand what data is collected: a random participant code, a broad role category and
      experience band, task outcomes with timings and assistance counts, my questionnaire
      ratings, and any optional comments I choose to write.
- [ ] I understand that my **name and contact details are not part of the research record**, and
      that my responses are identified only by the participant code.
- [ ] I understand that I may withdraw my responses by quoting my participant code within
      **14 days** of my session, and that after that point they are anonymised and can no longer
      be identified or removed.
- [ ] I understand that my data is stored in University-approved encrypted storage, accessible to
      the researcher and supervisor only, and retained until **degree award plus 12 months**.
- [ ] I understand that **anonymised** quotations and aggregate results may appear in the
      dissertation and in any resulting publication.
- [ ] I understand that the **software is being evaluated, not me**, and that a task I cannot
      complete is recorded as a result about the software.
- [ ] I agree to take part in this study.

**There is no recording consent item on this form, because no recording takes place.** If a future
study wanted recording, it would need a separate ethics application and its own consent item;
adding one here would misdescribe this study.

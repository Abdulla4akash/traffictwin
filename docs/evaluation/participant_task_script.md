# Participant Task Script

Status: draft; use only after applicable approval.

## Opening

TrafficTwin is an import-first research prototype. Synthetic examples are labelled synthetic.
Diagnostic outputs are hypotheses, not proven causes. We are evaluating the workflow, not you.

## Tasks

1. Open the Guided Demo and identify the current data mode.
2. Duplicate a synthetic scenario preset, change one parameter, and export or generate its bundle.
3. Validate the bundle and state whether it may enter analysis.
4. Compare the baseline and variation and identify one measured change.
5. Open Diagnostics & Evidence and explain one rule result and one missing-evidence item.
6. Open Provenance Explorer and trace one metric to its source context.
7. Open Triviality & Winner Map and explain why synthetic policy-profile results are not real
   algorithm evidence.
8. State one follow-up experiment you would perform before acting on a diagnostic hypothesis.

## Observer record

For each task record: `completed`, `completed_with_assistance`, `not_completed`, elapsed seconds,
assistance count, and a short non-identifying note. Do not record participant names in this sheet.

## Closing

Complete the approved survey or interview. Remind the participant of the withdrawal process and
provide the study contact details from the approved information sheet.

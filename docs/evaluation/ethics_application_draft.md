# Ethics Application Draft

Status: draft for supervisor review; not submitted and not approved.

## Study purpose

Evaluate whether researchers can use TrafficTwin's import-first workflow to author a scenario,
validate an imported result, inspect a comparison, understand evidence-linked diagnostic
hypotheses, and trace a displayed result to its provenance.

## Participants

Approximately 4–10 adult researchers or postgraduate students familiar with traffic simulation,
vehicular edge computing, or research software. Participation is voluntary. Unless actual traffic
operators take part, results will be described as expert/researcher feedback rather than end-user
validation.

## Procedure

One 30–45 minute task-based session using synthetic fixtures or material explicitly approved for
the study. The participant follows the task script and then completes either the survey or a
semi-structured interview. Screen or audio recording is disabled by default and requires separate
approval and consent.

## Data collected

- random participant code generated before the session;
- role category and broad experience bands;
- task completion, assistance count, and elapsed time;
- questionnaire ratings or coded interview notes;
- optional free-text comments after removal of names and organisation identifiers.

No name, email address, student/staff number, IP address, precise employer, or raw simulator secret
is part of the research result schema.

## Risks and mitigations

- Professional discomfort: make clear that the software, not the participant, is evaluated.
- Accidental disclosure: use synthetic/private-approved material and remove identifiers from notes.
- Withdrawal: participants may stop without giving a reason and may request deletion using their
  participant code until the stated anonymisation cutoff.
- Researcher power imbalance: avoid recruiting anyone whom the researcher directly assesses.

## Storage and retention

Store the participant-code key separately from response data, encrypted in University-approved
storage. Restrict access to the student researcher and named supervisor. Insert the approved
retention period and deletion date before submission.

## Analysis

Report task success counts, median or descriptive completion times, rating distributions, and
qualitative themes. Small samples will not be presented as population estimates. No LLM computes
metrics, codes participant findings, or invents conclusions.

## Approval fields still required

- final submission and study dates;
- approved recruitment route;
- survey versus interview decision;
- approved recording decision;
- retention period and storage location;
- withdrawal cutoff;
- supervisor and ethics reference numbers.

## Adversarial methodology pass

The fresh pass asked whether the prose could support a stronger claim than the source. It produced these clarifications or explicit retained limits:

- The actor chooses a mode, while environment targets and infrastructure execution/admission are separate decisions.
- Placement precedes the live gate in the frozen evaluator, regardless of a generic product diagram's ordering.
- The deadline gate excludes own service, radio and forwarding time; admission is not a feasibility guarantee.
- Task-count carry uses a fraction-of-served-work approximation and is not a per-job departure-event trace.
- Common-target retains three-pass vectorised reconciliation; per-task retains causal reservations. The comparison is not described as changing only an argmin-call frequency with all other algorithm semantics identical.
- The five internal task slots are not 200 ms ticks. Report-age reconstruction adds a continuous-service information model without redistributing arrivals.
- Fresh-state compatibility checks and positive-treatment validity checks have different purposes. Short tests do not establish a full historical rerun.
- Incident controls are reused, morning pilot seed 1 is excluded from primary inference, and the two scenario analyses are not pooled.
- Morning multiplicity adjustment is not retroactively assigned to historical incident intervals.
- The nominal 100 ms condition supplied unchanged information in this draw. It is not evidence of general staleness resilience.
- Positive forwarding outcomes are qualified record transformations. There were no full positive-cost reruns or measured physical backhaul results.
- Audit targets/endpoints are recorded; intermediate workloads are reconstructed. Spare alternatives do not establish a rescue count or complete causal attribution of the performance gap.
- The rotating-tie-break test remains proposed and unrun. No optional study is a manuscript-completion condition.
- No geographic generalisation, optimal-policy, real Kubernetes, complete remote backup or independent-review approval claim is introduced.

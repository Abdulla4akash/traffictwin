- Old “200 ms substep” comments and “fixed point” terminology were not edited. The frozen executable performs five ordered slots before one drain and exactly three mask updates; the revised explanation uses those semantics and does not assert convergence.

- The inherited batch-filling citation does not make the common-target destination rule equivalent to Ying et al.'s sequential update procedure.
- Bibliographic identity disagreements are documented in REFERENCE_CHECK.md. No historical result has been rewritten to match a preferred explanation.
- This revision recalculates compact archived counts/rates/intervals and checks source. It does not reopen the raw NPZ records for reanalysis, execute scientific validators, or reproduce simulations. All historical analysis receipts retain their original standing.
